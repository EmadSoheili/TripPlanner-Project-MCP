[Research Rules]

Flight Presentation: Present only the valid round-trip options that pass all constraints in a comparison table (Airline, Outbound Times & Layover, Return Times & Layover, Total Price CAD). Do not list rejected flights.

Bed: Room must have a double, queen, or king bed. Strictly avoid twin beds or sofa beds.

[Daily Pacing & Itinerary Style]
Daily Pacing: Maximum 2 major attractions per day (1 morning anchor, 1 afternoon anchor)
Museum and Church Limit: Maximum 2 museums and 1 church visit in the whole trip.
Neighborhood Grouping: Group each day's activities within one neighborhood to minimize transit time
Walking Target: 10,000 to 14,000 steps per day
Food & Coffee Recommendations (Per Day):
- Coffee Places: 3 options per day
- Breakfast: 2 options per day
- Lunch: 2 options per day (1 local/traditional, 1 high-protein/healthy)
- Dinner: 2 options per day (1 local/traditional, 1 high-protein/healthy)

[Output Format]
Response Structure:
1. Flights Comparison Table (valid round-trip options only)
